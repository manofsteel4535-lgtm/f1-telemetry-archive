## Validation boundaries

Maps and records are sourced, not independently surveyed or exhaustively re-audited. A later record may supersede a published source. The display is an offline snapshot, not live telemetry.

The Nürburgring counts as one venue; the Nordschleife is available in its historical configuration selector. No historical circuit is silently represented as the modern GP course.
